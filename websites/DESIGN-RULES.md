# Shared Design Rules

- Classic + modern.
- Clean + polished.
- Neither overloaded nor sparse.
- Comfortable for younger and older customers.
- Strong hierarchy and readable typography.
- Responsive desktop/mobile.
- Images should support the service, not create clutter.
- Do not accidentally turn images into navigation links.
- Do not visibly expose technical/source notes unless requested.
- Never fabricate testimonials, project history, prices, ownership or credentials.
