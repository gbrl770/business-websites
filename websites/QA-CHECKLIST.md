# Website QA Checklist

1. Correct index.html location.
2. Navigation anchors work.
3. Buttons/contact actions work.
4. Appointment/contact forms and generated messages work.
5. Language switching has no mixed-language content.
6. Mobile and desktop layout are sound.
7. Every image is real when requested, relevant, not an accidental link, and has no unwanted visible source/caption text.
8. Prices are internally consistent and clearly marked as estimates when they are market references.
9. Review UI works; do not claim shared persistence without a backend.
10. JavaScript syntax validation.
11. Check obvious runtime/console problems when possible.
12. Verify final ZIP/file structure.
13. Never claim QA passed unless actually performed.
